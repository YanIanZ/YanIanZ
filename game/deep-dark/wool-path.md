## 🧶 &nbsp;THE WOOL PATH

**`Y = -52`** &nbsp;·&nbsp; ❤️❤️❤️ &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🪵 pickaxe &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🧶 wool (7) &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🔥 torch

</div>

---

You lay wool one block at a time and step only on wool.

The sensors stay dark. It takes an eternity. Your torch dies halfway across and
you finish the crossing by touch alone, counting blocks in the black.

Seven blocks. Seven pieces of wool. You reach the central structure with none left
and both hands shaking.

The frame in front of you holds a **vault door**, sealed, and beside it a
narrow stair spiralling further down.

> 🧶 *Silent crossing. Nothing woke up. That's the good ending of this room.*
