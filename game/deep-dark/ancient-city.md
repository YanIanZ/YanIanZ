You drop onto the city floor. It is paved in **sculk**, and sculk remembers footsteps.

Ahead: the central structure, ribs of deepslate around a **redstone-lit frame**.
Between you and it, a plaza studded with **sculk sensors** — dozens of them,
tendrils twitching at every vibration.

To your left, a ruined stall holds a chest. On top of the chest sits a coil of
grey-white **wool**.
