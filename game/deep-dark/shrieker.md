## 📣 &nbsp;THE SHRIEKER

**`Y = -52`** &nbsp;·&nbsp; ❤️❤️❤️ &nbsp;·&nbsp; **DARKNESS III**

</div>

---

The scream rolls across the city and your vision **collapses** — the darkness
effect, pulsing in and out like a bad connection.

You count the shrieks. One. Two.

You know — everyone knows — what the **fourth** one does.

Through the strobing dark you can just make out two things: the vault door ahead,
and a low stair spiralling down to your right.
