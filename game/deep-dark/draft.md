---

You follow the moving air down a tunnel that was **not** carved by water.

The walls turn from deepslate to something darker. Something with **veins** in it
that pulse faintly blue when your torch passes.

Sculk.

The tunnel opens onto a balcony. Below you, impossibly, is a **city** — basalt
towers, cracked pillars, and a single glowing structure at the centre.
