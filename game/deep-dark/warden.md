The fourth shriek doesn't echo. It *lands*.

The floor forty blocks away bulges and splits, and something climbs out of it —
blind, antlered, chest glowing like a furnace door left open.

It does not run at you. It doesn't need to. It **sniffs**.

Your pickaxe is wood. Your armour is a shirt. Fighting is not on the menu.
