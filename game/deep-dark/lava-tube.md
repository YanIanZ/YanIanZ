## 🛤️ &nbsp;THE OLD RAILS

**`Y = -48`** &nbsp;·&nbsp; ❤️❤️ &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🪵 pickaxe (cracked) &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🔥 torch

</div>

---

The rails run for two hundred blocks and then simply **stop**, sheared off at the
lip of a lava tube.

The heat here is a physical wall. On the far side of the tube, ten blocks across,
the rails resume — and beside them sits a minecart chest, intact.

Below both of you: orange, moving, patient.
