You climb.

It takes hours. Y-59 to Y-52 to Y-11 to daylight, hand over hand, and when you
finally push through the last layer of dirt the sun is so bright it hurts.

Eight diamonds in your pocket. Both arms working. One story nobody will believe.

Behind you, somewhere very far down, something goes back to sleep.

> **You got out rich.** Most people don't get out at all.
