You put your back to the wall and do nothing for a full minute.

The breathing has a rhythm. Between breaths: a soft **click**, like a sensor
arming itself. You count. Four seconds. Every time.

Then you understand what you're hearing. It isn't breathing.

It's a **shrieker**, and it's waiting for a sound to report.

You now know the rule of this place: **it hunts noise, not light.**

> 🧠 *You've learned something. Sneaking is free. Running is fatal.*
