---

You go still. Truly still — the kind of still where you can hear your own pulse
in your teeth.

Somewhere behind you, something enormous stops, tilts its head, and walks the
other way.

You breathe again, and you open the vault.

Inside: **eight diamonds**, a stack of **echo shards**, and a book with no title.
The book's only page reads:

> *"He built the whole thing at 3 in the morning and it worked on the first try.
> We buried the server with him."*
