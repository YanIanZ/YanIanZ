## 🌀 &nbsp;THE UNFINISHED FRAME

**`Y = -64`** &nbsp;·&nbsp; ❤️❤️❤️ &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🪵 pickaxe

</div>

---

The stairs bottom out at bedrock, and bedrock should be the end of the world.

It isn't. Someone **cut into it**.

A portal frame stands in the cut — not obsidian, not crying obsidian, something
the game has no name for. Eleven blocks placed. One socket empty.

The empty socket is exactly the size of a **diamond**.
